## Our Mission

At Unixel Technologies, we believe technology should be simple, accessible, and open to everyone. Whether you're booting an operating system for the very first time or typing your first command in a terminal, our goal is the same: **make computers and tech as simple as possible.**

We take inspiration from the reliability and customer-first philosophy of **Dell Technologies**, the power and flexibility of **Unix**, the open spirit of **Linux**, and — for certain lightweight projects — even a touch of **Windows**-style usability (yes, including our mini HTML operating system concept).

> *"The key to .NET, the key to industry transformation, the key to success is developers, developers, developers..."*
> — Steve Ballmer, former Microsoft CEO

That quote is the heart of Unixel-CLI. Developers move the industry forward — so we build for them first.

## Why Open Systems?

At Unixel, we value open platforms over locked-down ones. Open systems teach you how things work, give you freedom to customize, and never hold your experience hostage. That philosophy runs through every project we build — and it's exactly why B-Box greets you with *Welcome*, not *Sign in*.

## Get Involved

Unixel is built in the open, and community feedback drives us forward.

### 🤝 Join Us

At Unixel Technologies, we're always open to new developers joining the team — or even a small one-time helping hand from a volunteer. Whether you want to contribute code, docs, ideas, or just a quick fix, there's a place for you here. Every contribution counts, no matter the size.

### 🛠️ How to Contribute

New to open source? No problem — here's the standard workflow, step by step:

1. **Fork** this repository to your own GitHub account (click the **Fork** button at the top right).
2. **Clone** your fork locally:

        git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/YOUR-FORK.git

3. **Create a new branch** for your change — one feature or fix per branch:

        git checkout -b my-awesome-feature

4. **Make your changes** — code, docs, examples, anything helpful.
5. **Commit** with a clear message:

        git commit -m "Add: short description of what you changed"

6. **Push** to your branch:

        git push origin my-branch-name

7. **Open a Pull Request** against this repository, and describe what you changed and why.

Not sure where to start? Check the **Issues** tab for open items, or just open a new issue and ask — we're happy to point you in the right direction. First-timers are more than welcome.

### 🐛 Found a problem?

Please report it using the **Issues** tab of this repository. To help us resolve it faster, include:

1. A clear title describing the problem
2. The steps to reproduce it
3. Any extra notes — screenshots, expected vs. actual behavior, environment details

The more detail you provide, the faster we can help.
